Custom Hover Trailer Settings

On computers with a mouse, plays the video preview directly inside the hovered movie/series poster card.
On TVs and remote/keyboard navigation, automatically plays the trailer inside the currently selected/focused card.
On phones and tablets, automatically plays the trailer on the card closest to the center of the screen when scrolling stops.
Swipe from left to right across any movie poster card on your phone to immediately play its trailer (and switch from any currently playing trailer). Allows playing cards near the very top or bottom of the page that never reach the center of the screen!
When enabled, disables automatic center-of-screen playback on phones so trailers only play when you swipe left-to-right on a card.
Instead of repeating only the first available trailer, cycles through every available trailer for the movie in your selected priority order (e.g., Local Trailer → Remote MP4 → YouTube #1 → YouTube #2 → back to first).
Time to wait after hovering over a card before starting the video preview (default: 400ms).
Length of the preview loop in seconds (default: 30s).
Recommended so browsers allow immediate autoplay on hover.
When unchecked, 16:9 trailers are letterboxed (object-fit: contain) strictly within the original poster dimensions.
